About M-⁠Pack

Molding Passionate Active Creative Kids.

M-⁠Pack is a nonprofit that provides trauma-informed afterschool care, helping young people build skills, relationships, and confidence. Outside the classroom and beyond.

Our mission

To provide life coaching intervention and other supports to address the social, emotional, and behavioral risk associated with Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs).

Our purpose

Above all, we wish that youth prosper and be in good health, outside the classroom and beyond.

M-⁠Pack serves school-age young people, including those in families facing major transitions or economic hardship. Our work centers on safe, nurturing spaces, caring adults, and activities that let young people discover who they are.
