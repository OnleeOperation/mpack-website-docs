# 01 — Product Brief

## Working product name

**M-Pack Digital Hub**

This is an internal working name, not necessarily a public brand name.

## Problem

M-Pack needs a stronger digital presence that can explain its work and support a more stable funding model.

The current class strategy uses three funding approaches:

- recurring corporate sponsorships,
- recurring monthly giving,
- craft/merch sales.

Without a central digital experience, these ideas remain separate. The website should connect them.

## Product thesis

A stronger online presence can turn curiosity into repeat engagement.

The intended visitor journey is:

```text
Discover M-Pack
      ↓
Understand the work
      ↓
See credible proof / activity
      ↓
Choose a way to engage
      ↓
Newsletter / Craft purchase / Monthly giving / Partnership
      ↓
Ongoing relationship
```

The craft strategy has its own journey:

```text
Discover a craft
      ↓
Understand the activity behind it
      ↓
Purchase
      ↓
Follow / subscribe
      ↓
Learn about the Donors' Club
      ↓
Potential recurring supporter
```

## Main user jobs

### Prospective donor
"I want to understand what M-Pack does and whether I trust it enough to support."

### Existing supporter
"I want to see what my support helps make possible and stay connected."

### Craft shopper
"I like this item, but I also want to know what my purchase supports."

### Corporate partner
"I want to understand M-Pack quickly and know how my company could get involved."

### Community member
"I want to learn about events, activities, and ways to stay involved."

## Primary conversion goals

1. recurring support,
2. newsletter signup / relationship capture,
3. craft and merch purchases,
4. corporate partnership inquiry,
5. event/community engagement.

## MVP success

The first version succeeds if a class audience can immediately understand:

- what M-Pack does,
- why recurring support matters,
- what the Donors' Club is,
- where crafts/merch fit,
- how one-time interest can become a longer relationship.
