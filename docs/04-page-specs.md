# 04 — Page Specifications

# Home `/`

## Goal
Move a first-time visitor from "I don't know M-Pack" to one clear action.

## Required modules
- Header
- Hero
- What M-Pack does
- Impact/activity proof
- Ways to support
- Featured crafts
- Donors' Club
- Corporate partnerships
- Newsletter
- Privacy/trust
- Footer

## Primary CTA
`Support M-Pack`

## Secondary CTA
`See Our Work`

---

# Our Work `/our-work`

## Goal
Explain what M-Pack actually does without jargon.

## Structure

### Intro
Short mission statement.

### Experience pillars
- coaching/guidance,
- new experiences,
- skill development,
- social connection.

### How an experience becomes growth
Use a simple visual explanation rather than a dense paragraph.

### Activities
Cards for approved activity categories such as:
- field trips,
- workshops,
- enrichment,
- mentorship,
- community events.

### CTA
`Help Fund the Next Experience`

---

# Impact `/impact`

## Goal
Build trust without overstating evidence.

## Sections
1. verified impact metrics,
2. what support funds,
3. recent program highlights,
4. one de-identified story if approved,
5. reporting/privacy approach,
6. CTA to recurring giving.

## Rule
No metric appears unless it is sourced and approved.

---

# Shop `/shop`

## Goal
Offer a simple, mission-connected retail experience.

## Categories
- Crafts
- M-Pack Merch

## Features
- product cards,
- category filter,
- optional search,
- cart or hosted checkout,
- clear shipping note,
- mission reminder,
- link to Donors' Club.

The shop should feel like M-Pack, not Etsy.

---

# Craft detail `/shop/[slug]`

## Required fields
- product name,
- image/illustrated placeholder,
- price,
- stock status,
- short description,
- activity-behind-the-craft story,
- material/care info if relevant,
- shipping note,
- buy CTA,
- newsletter CTA,
- optional soft Donors' Club CTA.

## Story rule
Do not identify a child or describe private personal history.

Good example:
> Created during an M-Pack workshop centered on creativity, patience, and working together.

Bad example:
> Created by [child name], age 10, who experienced [private adversity].

---

# Merch `/shop/merch`

## Goal
Create visible community identity around M-Pack.

Possible categories:
- shirts,
- hats,
- tote bags,
- stickers,
- small branded items.

Use placeholders until actual inventory is approved.

---

# Donors' Club `/donors-club`

## Goal
Make recurring giving simple, transparent, and worthwhile.

## Sections
1. why monthly giving matters,
2. how joining works,
3. approved tiers or custom monthly amount,
4. what members receive,
5. impact/reporting promise,
6. cancel/change language,
7. secure hosted checkout,
8. FAQ.

Do not promise perks M-Pack cannot fulfill.

---

# Partners `/partners`

## Goal
Turn corporate interest into a conversation.

## Sections
- why partner with M-Pack,
- ways to engage:
  - recurring sponsorship,
  - employee giving,
  - volunteer/event collaboration,
  - matching gifts if available,
- what M-Pack can provide:
  - impact updates,
  - employee engagement,
  - community visibility,
- approved partners/logos,
- inquiry form.

CTA: `Start a Partnership Conversation`

---

# Updates `/updates`

## Goal
Give M-Pack a credible ongoing presence.

Post types:
- program highlight,
- field trip recap,
- community event,
- donor update,
- craft drop,
- partner highlight.

Each post should include:
- date,
- category,
- short summary,
- privacy-safe content,
- CTA.

---

# About `/about`

Include only verified:
- organization overview,
- mission,
- leadership,
- history,
- service area,
- public contact info.

---

# Contact `/contact`

Fields:
- name,
- email,
- reason for contact,
- message.

Reason options:
- General
- Donate
- Corporate Partnership
- Volunteer
- Shop Order
- Media / Community
