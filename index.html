Our mission

To provide life coaching intervention and other supports to address the social, emotional, and behavioral risk associated with Adverse Childhood Experiences.

In everyday terms: after school, young people find a steady group of caring adults, a healthy meal, hands-on activities, and chances to try things they might never otherwise get to try.

How the program works

Why early support matters

Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) are stressful or traumatic events in childhood, and they are common: statewide reports from 2016–2018 found that 3 in 5 Georgians had experienced at least one.

“Creating safe, stable, nurturing relationships and environments can prevent ACEs and help all children reach their full health and life potential.” — CDC, 2024

What growth can look like

Four ways M-⁠Pack helps young people grow

Coaching & guidance

Consistent adults help young people reflect, set goals, and keep moving forward.

New experiences

Field trips, workshops, and enrichment open up new places, ideas, and possibilities.

Skill building

Hands-on activities create space to practice patience, creativity, communication, and follow-through.

Community & connection

Shared experiences strengthen relationships among students, mentors, volunteers, and supporters.
