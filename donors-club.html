Questions

Donors’ Club FAQ

Can I change or cancel my monthly gift?

Yes, anytime. Every receipt will include a link to update or cancel your gift. (Prototype: no real payments are taken.)

What do members receive?

Digital impact reports and a member newsletter at every level, with additional benefits at higher levels. Benefits shown here are proposals; M-⁠Pack will confirm only what it can fulfill.

How is my payment handled?

Payments are handled by a secure hosted checkout provider. This website never sees or stores card details.

Is the Donors’ Club a donation or a purchase?

Membership is a donation. Crafts and merch are purchases, and the two are kept separate.

Are gifts tax-deductible?

M-⁠Pack is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Please talk with your tax advisor about your situation; M-⁠Pack will confirm official receipt language before launch.
