# Brand voice

M-Pack should sound:

- hopeful,
- direct,
- grounded,
- community-focused,
- specific,
- respectful,
- active.

Avoid:

- pity,
- savior language,
- exaggerated promises,
- vague nonprofit buzzwords,
- trauma-heavy storytelling,
- treating children as marketing assets.

# What M-Pack does

## Heading
What growth can look like

### Coaching & Guidance
Consistent adults can help young people reflect, set goals, and keep moving forward.

### New Experiences
Field trips, workshops, and enrichment can expose students to new places, ideas, and possibilities.

### Skill Building
Hands-on activities create space to practice patience, creativity, communication, and follow-through.

### Community & Connection
Shared experiences can strengthen relationships among students, mentors, volunteers, and supporters.

# Privacy / safeguarding

## Heading
Kids' privacy comes first.

## Body
M-Pack should tell stories in a way that protects the young people it serves. Public content should focus on activities, progress, and community while using names and faces only when appropriate consent is documented.
