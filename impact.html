What we’re learning

What M-⁠Pack’s data tells us

Students build self-control

Most students in the program showed measurable improvement in self-control over the year.

Growth, even from hard starting points

Students develop positive behaviors over time, including those who begin from challenging baselines and higher-risk backgrounds.

Tracking shows what each student needs

Consistent behavior tracking helps coaches see patterns and tailor support to student-specific needs.

Peers and attention matter

The data shows peer influence and attention-seeking are key challenges, which is why M-⁠Pack builds positive group experiences.
