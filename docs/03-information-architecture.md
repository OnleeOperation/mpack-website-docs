# 03 — Information Architecture

## Recommended MVP navigation

Desktop:

```text
M-Pack logo
Our Work
Impact
Shop
Donors' Club
Partners
Updates
[Support M-Pack]
```

Mobile:

- logo,
- menu button,
- highly visible `Support M-Pack` CTA.

## Recommended route structure

```text
/
├── /our-work
├── /impact
├── /shop
│   ├── /crafts
│   ├── /merch
│   └── /[slug]
├── /donors-club
├── /partners
├── /updates
│   └── /[slug]
├── /about
├── /contact
├── /privacy
└── /terms
```

For the classroom MVP, separate pages may be collapsed into homepage sections if time is limited.

# Homepage section order

## 1. Header
Primary CTA: **Support M-Pack**

Secondary paths:
- Our Work
- Shop
- Donors' Club
- Partners

## 2. Hero
Job:
- explain M-Pack in one sentence,
- communicate possibility rather than crisis,
- provide one main CTA and one secondary CTA.

Suggested:

**Eyebrow:** M-Pack

**Headline:**
`More experiences. Stronger connections. More room to grow.`

**Body:**
M-Pack creates structured experiences, coaching, and opportunities that help young people build skills, relationships, and confidence.

**Primary CTA:** Support M-Pack
**Secondary CTA:** See Our Work

## 3. What M-Pack does
Use 3–4 cards:

- Coaching & guidance
- New experiences
- Skill building
- Community & connection

## 4. Impact / activity proof
Goal: answer the donor question: "What does support make possible?"

Use only verified metrics. If metrics are not approved yet, show qualitative activity proof:

- field trips,
- workshops,
- mentoring,
- enrichment,
- community experiences.

## 5. Ways to support
Four cards:

1. Give Monthly
2. Shop Crafts
3. Shop M-Pack Merch
4. Become a Corporate Partner

## 6. Featured Craft Initiative

Headline:
`Made through M-Pack experiences. Purchased to support more of them.`

Show 3–4 product cards.

Each card:
- craft thumbnail,
- product name,
- short activity-level story,
- price,
- `View Craft`.

Do not use child names.

## 7. Donors' Club

Headline:
`Make support predictable.`

Explain:
- monthly giving,
- more consistent planning,
- updates,
- approved member benefits.

CTA: `Explore the Donors' Club`

## 8. Corporate partnerships

Headline:
`Put your company behind local youth.`

Explain:
- sponsorship,
- employee giving,
- volunteer engagement,
- events.

CTA: `Explore Partnerships`

## 9. Newsletter / community

Headline:
`Stay close to the work.`

Fields:
- first name optional,
- email required.

Promise:
- program updates,
- upcoming events,
- new crafts/merch,
- ways to help.

## 10. Privacy / trust signal

Headline:
`Kids' privacy comes first.`

Explain:
- de-identified stories where needed,
- activity-focused storytelling,
- consent-first use of faces.

## 11. Footer

Include:
- M-Pack logo,
- short mission line,
- navigation,
- public contact email,
- newsletter link/form,
- social links,
- privacy/terms.
