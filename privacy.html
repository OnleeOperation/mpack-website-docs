How we protect young people

This website tells the story of M-⁠Pack’s work, not the private histories of individual children.

  • We don’t publish children’s full names, schools, home locations, or family details.
  • We don’t share diagnoses, behavioral records, or case notes.
  • Stories are told at the activity level, and names are changed when a story is shared.
  • Photos favor spaces, crafts, hands, and activities. A face appears only with written guardian consent that covers public web use.
  • Crafts are sold by M-⁠Pack. We never identify the child who made an item, and buyers can’t contact children.

Meet Mul-T, our mascot: he represents an archetype of our participants so we can share what the program feels like without sharing anyone’s details.

Information we collect from visitors

  • Newsletter: your email address and, optionally, your first name.
  • Contact and partner forms: your name, email, and message (plus company and role for partnership inquiries).
  • Payments: handled entirely by secure third-party checkout providers. This website never sees or stores card details.

Please don’t submit information about any child through this website’s forms. We use the minimum information needed to respond to you, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time.

Analytics

We aim to use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits and clicks without tracking individuals. We never send form contents, payment details, or any information about children to analytics.

Questions

Contact us through the contact form with any privacy question or to request removal of your information.
