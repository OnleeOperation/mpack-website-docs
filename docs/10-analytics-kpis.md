# 10 — Analytics and KPIs

The website should make the funding strategy measurable.

# Primary funnel

```text
Visit
  ↓
Understand M-Pack
  ↓
Engage
  ↓
Newsletter / Shop / Donors' Club / Partner Inquiry
  ↓
Repeat engagement
```

# Website KPIs

## Awareness
- unique visitors,
- traffic source,
- landing-page engagement,
- scroll depth,
- returning visitors.

## Newsletter
- signup conversion rate,
- source page,
- email confirmation rate if double opt-in,
- unsubscribe rate later.

## Donors' Club
- CTA click rate,
- checkout starts,
- recurring donor conversions,
- recurring donor retention if provider data exists.

## Craft / merch
- product views,
- add-to-cart or checkout clicks,
- conversion rate,
- revenue,
- average order value,
- best-performing products,
- repeat buyers if available.

## Corporate
- partner page views,
- inquiry starts,
- completed inquiries,
- meetings generated.

# Craft Initiative pilot metrics

At community events and online, track:

- units sold,
- revenue,
- direct product cost,
- estimated margin,
- event/channel,
- products viewed vs purchased,
- QR scans,
- newsletter signups,
- Donors' Club clicks/signups,
- repeat engagement.

This is what makes craft sales more than a one-time fundraiser.

# Event QR tracking

For community events, use event-specific QR codes with UTM parameters.

Example:

```text
?utm_source=community_event
&utm_medium=qr
&utm_campaign=craft_pilot
&utm_content=[event-name]
```

# Dashboard concept

A simple monthly dashboard could show:

1. website visitors,
2. newsletter growth,
3. monthly donor conversions,
4. shop revenue,
5. corporate leads,
6. top-performing content.

Do not build a dashboard in the MVP unless time allows.
